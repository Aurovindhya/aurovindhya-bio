About me

I'm an AI Software Engineer at Recollective Inc. in Ottawa, specialising in production-grade AI systems: retrieval-augmented generation pipelines, agentic workflows, and rigorous LLM evaluation. I bring a full-stack background to every layer of the stack, from data modelling through to deployed inference.

I hold a Master of Engineering in Data Science from the University of Ottawa, which grounds my engineering work in solid mathematical and statistical thinking. I care deeply about systems that are not only accurate, but observable, testable, and maintainable in production.

Outside of AI, I've shipped a native Android app (GratitudeJar), built ML classification systems, and keep a running collection of experiments across computer vision, NLP, and recommender systems.
